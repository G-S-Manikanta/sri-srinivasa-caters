At Sri Srinivasa Caters, we believe that an auspicious meal is the divine soul of every traditional South Indian wedding.

For over two decades, our master chefs have preserved the sacred culinary secrets of Andhra, Tamil, and Karnataka traditions — using unadulterated pure cow ghee, stone-ground masalas, and heirloom techniques to craft feasts that evoke warmth, sanctity, and unforgettable joy.

“When food is prepared with reverence and served with an open heart, it transforms an occasion into an everlasting blessing.” Master Chef & Founder, Sri Srinivasa Caters
